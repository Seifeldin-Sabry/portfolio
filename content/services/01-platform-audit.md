# Platform audit

A focused review of your codebase and delivery pipeline by the engineer who owns the Qfacts platform end to end. You get a written report with prioritised, concrete fixes.

## Includes

- Repo structure, type safety and API contracts (monorepos, generated SDKs)
- CI/CD and release process: what breaks, what's slow, what's risky
- GCP infrastructure as code (Pulumi), deploy safety and recovery
- Observability: logging, alerting, error tracking
