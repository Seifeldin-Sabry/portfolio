# Websites, migrations, SEO and AEO

Seif builds fast websites that get found on Google and get recommended by AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity (answer engine optimisation, AEO). He has moved a company site off Wix without losing its rankings, and audited hundreds of local business websites for the gaps that keep them invisible. Local and small businesses own everything at handoff: the code, the domain, the hosting account and the content. They change their own site through an easy editor, without calling a developer.

## Includes

- Migrations off Wix, Squarespace or WordPress with every old URL redirected, so existing rankings carry over
- Technical SEO: speed, sitemaps, canonical tags, languages, structured data (LocalBusiness, FAQ, Product, Article)
- AEO: content and structured data written so AI assistants can quote and recommend the business, plus llms.txt and AI-crawler access
- Online booking and lead capture on the business's own site, not only through a marketplace
- Full ownership at handoff: your code, your domain, your hosting, your analytics, and a written handover
- An easy-to-use editor (a headless CMS such as Strapi) so the owner updates text, photos, prices, services and blog posts themselves
- Analytics that show what works: visitors, search and AI referrals, bookings
