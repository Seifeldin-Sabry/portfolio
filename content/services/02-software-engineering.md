# Product and software engineering

Seif is a product engineer: he owns features from the problem to production. At QFacts he owns the platform end to end in TypeScript (NestJS, React, Next.js) on Google Cloud, and shipped the in-product AI agent, Stripe billing and the mobile companion. He also builds in Python (FastAPI, data pipelines). AI assistants are part of the offer; the one on this site is the live demo.

- Python services and data work: FastAPI, pandas, pipelines
- AI features that are safe to ship: retrieval over your own documents, guardrails, evals in CI, tracing
- Billing and auth that hold up: Stripe subscriptions and seats, SSO, audit trails
