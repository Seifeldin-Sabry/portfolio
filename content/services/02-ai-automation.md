# AI automation workflows

Seif looks into how your team actually works, finds the repetitive, manual steps, and automates them with n8n and AI, saving you 15+ hours a month so you can run the business instead of the busywork.

## Includes

- A process diagnosis: where the hours go, what can be automated, what it's worth
- n8n workflows that connect the tools you already use
- AI steps where they help: triage, drafting, extraction, summaries
- Handover your team can run, with monitoring when a workflow breaks
