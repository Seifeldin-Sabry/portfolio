# Founder — SDX Studio

SDX Studio is a GTM engineering agency that runs done-for-you outbound. Its first niche is MSSPs (managed security service providers), and the playbook is built to be reused for any B2B niche. The MSSP offer is simple: 6 qualified appointments in 90 days, and SDX keeps working for free until they're delivered. Website: https://sdxstudio.co

## Achievements

- Runs the whole outbound motion from Claude Code: lead scraping, enrichment, copy and runbooks as versioned playbooks and custom skills
- Built multichannel outreach for security buyers: LinkedIn (HeyReach) and CISO-targeted cold email (Smartlead)
- Treats the business like a codebase: offer, contract, copy and playbooks live in one repo with a changelog, so the motion improves with every iteration
- Signal-based sourcing (job posts, hiring spikes, funding) with Apify, Exa and Hunter, AI-drafted personal messages and human approval before anything is sent
- Pointed the same pipeline at three niches: security buyers, SaaS teams and local businesses
