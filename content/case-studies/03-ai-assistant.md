# An AI assistant a regulated product can trust

## Problem

Quality teams spend hours searching documents and drafting from a blank page, and recurring problems get logged again instead of fixed. But in a GAMP 5 validated product, an AI that confidently makes things up is worse than no AI.

## What I did

- Delivered an AI assistant with retrieval over each customer's own documents, plus brainstorming and similarity checks across quality events.
- Kept humans in control: document revisions are staged as drafts that require human review.
- Backed it with durable jobs, execution tracing and evaluation experiments.

## Outcome

- Less time spent searching and drafting.
- Auditors and workers spot recurring issues early, so root causes get addressed instead of logged again.
