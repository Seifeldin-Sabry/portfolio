# Why local businesses stay invisible to Google and ChatGPT

## Problem

Dentists, clinics, physios, renovation firms and estate agents with hundreds of good reviews still lose patients and clients to competitors with worse service and better websites. AI assistants now answer "best dentist near me" questions directly, and they lean on structured data and clear answers on the business's own site.

## What I did

- Pulled every business with 15 or more Google reviews in five niches across Antwerp, London and Sarajevo.
- Loaded each website and checked it for what Google and AI assistants read: LocalBusiness and FAQ structured data, meta description, H1, speed, analytics, online booking on the business's own site, and site-builder errors.
- Scored each business and wrote a one-line fix for the biggest gap.

## Outcome

- 69% of the 681 live sites had no LocalBusiness structured data.
- 94% had no FAQ structured data, the format AI assistants quote most easily.
- 38% offered no online booking on their own website.
- 59 businesses with many reviews had no website at all, and 9 had a broken one.
